## App Overview

Desti is a campus transport app for verified Stetson University students. It connects drivers offering rides with passengers who need them. All users must have a valid @stetson.edu email address to use the platform.

## How to Create a Ride

If you are a driver with available seats, you can post a ride:

1. Go to the "Post a Ride" section.
2. Enter your origin and destination (e.g., "Library" → "Walmart").
3. Set your departure window (earliest and latest departure times).
4. Choose a distance category (SHORT, MEDIUM, or LONG).
5. Set your price in dollars (e.g., $4.00).
6. Specify how many seats are available.
7. Optionally add pickup and dropoff instructions.
8. Submit the ride.

Your ride will appear as ACTIVE and visible to other students looking for transport.

## How to Create a Trip Request

If you need a ride but none are available, you can post a trip request:

1. Go to the "Request a Trip" section.
2. Enter where you need to go (origin and destination).
3. Set the time window you'd like to depart.
4. Choose the distance category.
5. Specify how many seats you need.
6. Submit your request.

Drivers can browse trip requests and send you an offer.

## How Booking Works

- **Ride-based booking**: When a ride is posted, passengers can book available seats directly. The booking is confirmed immediately if seats are available.
- **Offer-based booking**: When a trip request is posted, a driver can send an offer. If the rider accepts the offer, a booking is created.

Each booking records how many seats were reserved and links to the ride or trip request.

## How Offers Work

- A driver can send an offer to a trip request, specifying how many seats they'll provide and at what price.
- The rider who posted the trip request can accept or decline the offer.
- Accepting an offer creates a confirmed booking.
- Only one offer per driver per trip request is allowed.

## Editing Rides and Trip Requests

- You can edit your ride or trip request before it has any confirmed bookings.
- Editable fields include origin, destination, departure times, price, seats, and instructions.
- Once a booking is confirmed against your ride or trip request, editing is locked.

## Cancellation Rules

- **Ride cancellation**: A driver can cancel their ride. If confirmed bookings exist, they will be cancelled too.
- **Booking cancellation**: A passenger can cancel their own booking.
- **Offer cancellation**: A driver can cancel a pending offer they sent.

## Dashboard

Your dashboard shows a summary of:

- Active rides you are driving
- Confirmed bookings (both ride-based and trip-request-based)
- Pending offers you have sent
- Pending offers you have received

## FAQ

**Q: Who can use Desti?**
A: Only verified Stetson University students with a @stetson.edu email.

**Q: Is there a fee to use the app?**
A: The platform itself is free. Drivers set their own prices for rides.

**Q: Can I create both rides and trip requests?**
A: Yes, you can be both a driver and a passenger.

**Q: What happens if no one books my ride?**
A: Your ride stays active until the latest departure time passes, then it becomes stale and is no longer shown.

**Q: How do I know if my booking is confirmed?**
A: The booking status will show as CONFIRMED in your dashboard.
